Keep watching a stream and follow the conversation while you browse, work, or take notes. **Video + chat PiP** brings YouTube’s player and interactive live chat into one window that stays above your other windows.

Move it to a convenient corner and keep watching. You can still read and reply in chat without switching back to the YouTube tab.

## Open it from the chat menu

:::media-left

![The Chat Enhancer menu with the Video + chat PiP option visible. All chat names, avatars, and messages are fictional.](./menu.png) {shadow=smooth}

On a YouTube watch page with live chat open:

1. Click the **Chat Enhancer** icon at the top of chat.
2. Choose **Video + chat PiP**.

:::

## A small window that makes room for both

The window opens with the video above a compact chat. Resize it to suit your screen; make it wider and the video and chat sit side by side.

![A wide picture-in-picture window with video and a fictional live chat side by side.](./pip-wide.png) {shadow=smooth}

:::media-right

![A compact picture-in-picture window with YouTube’s player controls above a fictional chat conversation.](./pip-stacked.png)

### Familiar controls, right there

Pause, adjust the volume, change quality, seek through a replay, or turn on captions when the video supports them. YouTube’s player controls stay with the video, and your Chat Enhancer tools stay with chat.

Chat uses more compact spacing while it is in the floating window. Your usual chat density setting stays saved for when you return.

:::

## Back to the tab in one click

Close the floating window, or choose **Return to tab** in the original video or chat area. Both return to their places on YouTube, and your unsent chat draft comes with you.

Keep the original YouTube tab open while you use PiP. Leaving the stream ends the floating session.

## Browser support

Video + chat PiP is available in supported desktop versions of **Chrome, Edge, and Firefox**. Safari does not currently support this combined video-and-chat window, so the option stays hidden there.
